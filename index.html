How this works: a “bathtub” model. Each spot’s ground elevation (≈10 m resolution in the US from USGS 3DEP via AWS Terrain Tiles) is compared to the water level you pick. It ignores levees, sea walls, drainage, waves and whether low inland spots actually connect to the water, so treat results as a stress test, not a flood-risk rating. Building footprints and heights: Overture Maps. Address search: OpenStreetMap Nominatim.
