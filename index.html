How this works: water level is measured from sea level (NAVD88). Ground elevations for the property and each building come from USGS 3DEP (lidar-based, 1–10 m) where available; the blue map shading uses coarser AWS Terrain Tiles, so it can differ slightly, especially below sea level. Elevation only floods every lower spot; NOAA uses NOAA's sea-level-rise maps, which only flood low areas connected to the sea (0–10 ft above local high tide). FEMA flood zones, 100-year flood levels and levees come from FEMA's National Flood Hazard Layer, converted to NAVD88 with NOAA VDatum. "Assume levees hold" treats FEMA levee-protected areas as dry. Sea walls aren't mapped nationally; FEMA and NOAA maps reflect them only where they shaped the official study. A stress test, not an insurance or engineering rating. Building footprints and heights: Overture Maps. Address search: OpenStreetMap Nominatim.
